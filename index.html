Supported by

The Future of Life Institute

The Future of Life Institute (FLI) is an independent nonprofit working to steer transformative technology toward benefiting life and away from extreme large-scale risks. Founded in 2014, FLI has become one of the most influential voices in AI safety, biosecurity, and existential risk research globally.

The Global AI Debates is proud to be supported by FLI. Their grant makes it possible to award prizes to outstanding students, subsidise registration for teams from under-resourced schools, and expand access to the competition worldwide. FLI has also expressed interest in collaborating on future debate topics, ensuring the competition continues to reflect the most important questions in AI safety and governance.

MT
Dr. Max Tegmark
Co-founder & President, Future of Life Institute
Max Tegmark is a physicist and AI safety researcher at MIT, and one of the world's leading voices on the long-term implications of artificial intelligence. He is the author of Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence, and a founding signatory of major AI safety initiatives including the 2023 open letter calling for a pause on giant AI experiments. His work bridges cutting-edge science and urgent questions about humanity's future.
